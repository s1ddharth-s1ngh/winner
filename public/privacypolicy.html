Privacy Policy

Winner for Life is a private challenge app for a small group of friends.

Health data

With your permission the app reads two things from Health Connect: your daily step count and your sleep sessions (when you fell asleep, when you woke up and how long you slept). It never writes to Health Connect.

These values are stored in the app's database and are visible only to you and to the members of the groups you join, to build the rankings. They are never sold, shared with third parties or used for advertising.

Your control

You can revoke access at any time from the Health Connect settings. You can leave a group at any time; you can ask the group owner to delete your account and data.
